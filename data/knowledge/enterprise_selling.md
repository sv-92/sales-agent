# Enterprise Sales Methodology

## The Enterprise Sales Cycle

### Stages and Activities

#### 1. Prospecting (Weeks 1-2)
- Identify target accounts using ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
- Research the organization: recent news, strategic priorities, tech stack
- Map key stakeholders and reporting structures
- Craft personalized outreach with relevant business triggers

#### 2. Discovery (Weeks 2-4)
- Conduct 2-3 discovery calls with different stakeholders
- Understand current state, desired state, and gap
- Identify decision-making unit (DMU) and process
- Qualify using MEDDIC or BANT framework

#### 3. Solution Design (Weeks 4-6)
- Build custom demo environment with their data/scenarios
- Create a technical architecture diagram for their environment
- Develop ROI model with their specific numbers
- Prepare security and compliance documentation

#### 4. Proposal and Negotiation (Weeks 6-10)
- Present formal proposal with pricing options
- Navigate procurement and legal review
- Handle final objections and stakeholder concerns
- Negotiate terms while protecting value

#### 5. Close and Handoff (Weeks 10-12)
- Finalize contract and signatures
- Introduce implementation team
- Set success metrics and review cadence
- Begin onboarding and change management

## Account Planning

### Strategic Account Framework

#### Territory Prioritization
- **Tier 1** (Top 20%): Named accounts with dedicated strategy
- **Tier 2** (Middle 30%): Growth accounts with quarterly touch
- **Tier 3** (Bottom 50%): Inbound-led, self-service where possible

#### Account Growth Strategies
1. **Land and Expand**: Start with one department, grow to enterprise license
2. **Cross-sell**: Introduce complementary products to existing customers
3. **Up-sell**: Migrate from standard to premium tiers
4. **Referral**: Turn champions into advocates for new accounts

### Relationship Mapping
For every strategic account, document:
- Economic buyer (budget authority)
- Technical evaluator (vetting solution)
- Champion (internal advocate)
- Coach (provides inside information)
- Blocker (opposes change or your solution)

## Metrics That Matter

### Pipeline Health Indicators
| Metric | Healthy Range | Warning Sign |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| Pipeline Coverage | 3-4x quota | Below 2.5x |
| Win Rate | 25-35% | Below 20% |
| Average Deal Size | Trending up | Shrinking |
| Sales Cycle Length | Consistent | Elongating |
| Stage Conversion | Progressive | Bunching |

### Activity Metrics
- 15-20 meaningful prospect conversations per week
- 3-5 new opportunities created per month
- 2-3 proposals delivered per month
- Pipeline reviews with manager weekly

## Common Enterprise Selling Mistakes

1. **Selling too low** — talking to users instead of buyers
2. **Single-threaded** — relying on one champion without backup
3. **Demo too early** — showing product before understanding needs
4. **Ignoring procurement** — starting legal/security review too late
5. **No mutual action plan** — letting deals drift without clear next steps
