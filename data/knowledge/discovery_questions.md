# Discovery Questions Framework

## Initial Qualification Questions

### Understanding the Problem
- "What prompted you to look for a solution now?"
- "What's the business impact of this problem today?"
- "How long has this been a challenge for your team?"
- "What happens if you don't solve this in the next 6 months?"

### Budget and Authority
- "Do you have a budget allocated for this type of solution?"
- "Who else is involved in evaluating and approving solutions like this?"
- "What's your typical procurement process and timeline?"
- "Have you invested in similar solutions before?"

### Current State Assessment
- "What tools or processes are you using today to address this?"
- "What's working well with your current approach?"
- "What are the biggest gaps or pain points?"
- "How many people are affected by this problem?"

## Deepening the Conversation

### Quantifying Impact
- "Can you estimate the hours lost per week due to this problem?"
- "What's the revenue impact of not having this capability?"
- "How does this affect your team's ability to hit their targets?"
- "If you solved this tomorrow, what would be different in 90 days?"

### Technical Requirements
- "What systems does this need to integrate with?"
- "Do you have specific security or compliance requirements?"
- "What does your current tech stack look like?"
- "Who would be the day-to-day users of this solution?"

### Success Criteria
- "How will you measure whether this solution is successful?"
- "What does 'good' look like 6 months after implementation?"
- "What are your must-have vs nice-to-have requirements?"
- "Have you defined specific KPIs for this project?"

## MEDDIC Framework Application

| Element | Question Focus |
|---------|---------------|
| Metrics | "What KPIs will improve?" |
| Economic Buyer | "Who signs off on this budget?" |
| Decision Criteria | "How will you evaluate options?" |
| Decision Process | "Walk me through your buying process" |
| Identify Pain | "What's the cost of inaction?" |
| Champion | "Who internally advocates for change?" |

## Tips for Effective Discovery

1. **Listen more than you talk** — aim for 70/30 ratio
2. **Take detailed notes** — reference specifics in follow-ups
3. **Ask open-ended questions** — avoid yes/no questions
4. **Follow up on vague answers** — "Can you tell me more about that?"
5. **Summarize and confirm** — "So what I'm hearing is..."
